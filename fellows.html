Advisory Members & Fellows

Scholars who help steer the Institute's inquiry.

Advisory Fellow

Ian J. MacDonald

University of Waterloo

An educator and writer at the University of Waterloo who investigates the interrelations between inquiry, meaning, and method — with a focus on AI ethics, the ethics of belief, and American pragmatism. He is co-author, with Matt Silk, of An Inquirer's Guide to Ethics in AI.

Advisory Fellow

David L. Hildebrand

University of Colorado Denver

Professor of Philosophy at the University of Colorado Denver. His current research examines the aesthetic, pragmatic, and ethical implications of digital and AI technologies. A past president of the Society for the Advancement of American Philosophy, he is the author of Beyond Realism and Antirealism and Dewey: A Beginner's Guide.
