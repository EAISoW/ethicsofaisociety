About the Ethics of AI Institute

The Ethics of AI Institute is an independent interdisciplinary organization serving as a forum for research, education, public engagement, and professional dialogue concerning the development, deployment, and governance of AI systems. Its mission is to promote informed and constructive engagement with the ethical challenges and opportunities presented by artificial intelligence through research, education, publications, events, and collaboration across academic, public, and private sectors.

Papers in this series are born of the Institute’s roundtable meetings, which convene scholars, practitioners, students, and community members across disciplines.
