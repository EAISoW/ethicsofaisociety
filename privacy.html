Institute Meeting Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Ethics of AI Institute Meetings

Purpose

The Ethics of AI Institute hosts regular meetings to facilitate dialogue, knowledge sharing, and collaborative exploration of topics related to the ethics, governance, and societal impacts of artificial intelligence (AI). These meetings are conducted through online meeting and collaboration platforms, and may be recorded.

This Privacy Policy explains how information shared during these meetings is collected, used, stored, and disclosed.

Participation and Recording

Meetings hosted by the Ethics of AI Institute may be conducted using online meeting and collaboration platforms. Meetings may be recorded, and recordings may be transcribed using platform-provided or third-party transcription services.

By participating in a meeting, attendees acknowledge that:

  • Audio, video, screen sharing, meeting attendance, chat messages, and other meeting interactions may be captured in the recording.
  • Participants who choose to enable their camera or attend in-person may appear in the recording.
  • Statements, comments, questions, and contributions made during the meeting may be included in the recording and transcript.
  • Participants may choose to attend online, disable their camera or refrain from speaking if they do not wish to be recorded.

By choosing to participate in a meeting after being notified of this Privacy Policy, attendees acknowledge that the collection, recording, storage, and use of meeting content will occur as described in this Policy.

Storage and Access

Meeting recordings, transcripts, and related materials are stored using systems and services authorized by the Ethics of AI Institute for collaboration, recordkeeping, research, and knowledge-sharing purposes.

Access to recordings and transcripts is generally restricted to individuals with authorized access through Institute-approved systems and services and may include Institute staff, researchers, affiliated collaborators, invited guests, and meeting participants.

Recordings and transcripts are maintained for administrative, educational, research, and knowledge-sharing purposes and will be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support those activities.
