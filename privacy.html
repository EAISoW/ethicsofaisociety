Institute Privacy Policy

Purpose and Scope

The Ethics of AI Institute brings together a community of members interested in the ethics, governance, and societal impacts of artificial intelligence (AI). The Institute supports this community by providing opportunities for members to connect and exchange ideas through online community platforms, virtual and in-person meetings, and email communications, including newsletters and updates for members who choose to subscribe.

In facilitating these activities, the Institute may collect, use, store and share personal information, including information provided through membership and community platforms, participation in meetings and discussions, and subscription to email communications.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Institute handles personal information in connection with these activities, including how meeting content may be recorded and used, how member information is managed, and how email subscriber information is collected and used.

Participation in Institute activities and subscription to email communications are distinct. Membership or participation in Institute’s community does not automatically constitute consent to receive newsletters or other optional email communications.

Meeting Participation and Recording

Meetings hosted by the Ethics of AI Institute may be conducted using online meeting and collaboration platforms. Meetings may be recorded, and recordings may be transcribed using platform-provided or third-party transcription services.

By participating in a meeting, attendees acknowledge that:

  • Audio, video, screen sharing, meeting attendance, chat messages, and other meeting interactions may be captured in the recording.
  • Participants who choose to enable their camera or attend in-person may appear in the recording.
  • Statements, comments, questions, and contributions made during the meeting may be included in the recording and transcript.
  • Participants may choose to attend online, disable their camera or refrain from speaking if they do not wish to be recorded.

By choosing to participate in a meeting after being notified of this Privacy Policy, attendees acknowledge that the collection, recording, storage, and use of meeting content will occur as described in this Policy.

Storage and Access to Meeting Materials

Meeting recordings, transcripts, and related materials are stored using systems and services authorized by the Ethics of AI Institute for collaboration, recordkeeping, research, and knowledge-sharing purposes.

Access to recordings and transcripts is generally restricted to individuals with authorized access through Institute-approved systems and services and may include Institute staff, researchers, affiliated collaborators, invited guests, and meeting participants.

Recordings and transcripts are maintained for administrative, educational, research, and knowledge-sharing purposes and will be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support those activities.

Public Meeting Summaries and Publications

Information from discussions may contribute to short-form articles and commentaries, roundtable reports or articles, white papers, research reports, educational materials, policy recommendations, and other publications produced by the Institute.

When preparing public summaries and publications:

  • Comments and discussion points will not be attributed to specific individuals unless the individual has provided explicit permission or the attribution is otherwise appropriate and agreed upon.
  • The Institute will make reasonable efforts to avoid including personally identifying information in publicly published summaries.
  • Meeting contributions may be paraphrased, summarized, or incorporated into publications without identifying individual participants, where appropriate.

Membership and Online Community

The Institute may collect and maintain personal information provided by individuals when they apply for or participate in Institute membership, create an account on an Institute community platform, or otherwise engage with the Institute. This may include names, email addresses, professional affiliations, profile information, and other information voluntarily provided by members.

The Institute may also collect information about members' participation in its online community, including posts, comments, messages, and other contributions. This information is used to administer membership, facilitate discussion and collaboration, provide access to Institute activities, and support the Institute's community and research-related objectives.

Information shared through the online community may be visible to other members or platform users. Members should exercise discretion when sharing personal or confidential information through these platforms.

Newsletter Subscriptions and Email Communications

The Ethics of AI Institute offers an optional newsletter subscription service to individuals who wish to receive updates about the Institute's activities, events, research, publications, and other news related to the ethics, governance, and societal impacts of AI.

Individuals who subscribe to the newsletter provide their email address for the purpose of receiving these communications. The Institute may also collect basic subscription-related information, such as subscription preferences, subscription status, and the date and method of subscription.

Newsletter subscription information is used to:

  • Distribute newsletters, announcements, and updates from the Institute.
  • Manage and maintain the newsletter subscriber list.
  • Administer subscription preferences and unsubscribe requests.
  • Monitor email delivery and engagement, including whether emails are opened and links are clicked, and collect related technical information such as IP addresses and timestamps, where available through the email distribution service.

This engagement information may be used to evaluate the effectiveness of the Institute's communications, understand subscriber engagement, improve future communications, and monitor the delivery and performance of email services.

Newsletter subscription is voluntary. Providing an email address for this purpose does not constitute registration for Institute meetings or consent to the recording of meeting participation.

The Institute retains subscriber information for as long as an individual's newsletter subscription remains active. Subscribers may withdraw their consent and unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link included in Institute email communications or by contacting the Institute directly.

Following an unsubscribe request, the Institute will remove the individual from its active subscriber list and cease sending newsletter communications. The Institute may retain limited information, such as an email address and unsubscribe status, where necessary to maintain a record of the request and prevent further communications from being sent inadvertently or to comply with applicable legal obligations.

The Institute will not sell or rent newsletter subscriber email addresses or use them for unrelated purposes.

Third-Party services

The Institute uses third-party service providers to support its activities, including meeting platforms, recording and transcription services, collaboration tools, data storage services, and email distribution services. These providers may process personal information on behalf of the Institute to provide their services, subject to applicable contractual and privacy obligations.

For newsletter distribution, the Institute uses Brevo, a third-party email marketing and communications service. Subscriber information, including email addresses and related subscription information, is shared with Brevo to manage the subscriber list and distribute Institute communications. Brevo may also process information relating to email delivery and engagement, as described in this Policy.

These service providers process personal information on behalf of the Institute in accordance with their applicable terms, privacy policies, and contractual arrangements. The use of these services may involve the processing or storage of personal information outside of Canada, depending on the provider's infrastructure and practices.

Personal Information, Privacy, and Individual Requests

The Institute takes reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Individuals may contact the Institute to:

  • Ask questions about how their personal information is collected, used, or stored.
  • Request access to or correction of their personal information, where applicable.
  • Unsubscribe from Institute email communications.
  • Raise concerns about the handling of their personal information or request the removal or de-identification of content in public summaries or publications.

Requests concerning meeting recordings, transcripts, summaries, and publications will be considered on a case-by-case basis, taking into account the purpose of the material, applicable obligations, and the rights and interests of others.

Policy Updates

The Ethics of AI Institute may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in its practices, technologies, legal requirements, or organizational activities. The most current version will be made available to participants.

Questions and Contact

Individuals may contact the Ethics of AI Institute with questions about this Privacy Policy or concerns regarding the handling of their personal information.
