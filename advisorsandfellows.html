Ethics of AI Institute Advisory Members and Fellows

Ian J. MacDonald

University of Waterloo
Advisory Fellow

Ian MacDonald is an educator and writer at the University of Waterloo who investigates the interrelations between inquiry, meaning, and method, with a focus on AI ethics, the ethics of belief, and American pragmatism. He is co-author, with Matt Silk, of An Inquirer’s Guide to Ethics in AI.

David L. Hildebrand

University of Colorado Denver
Advisory Fellow

David L. Hildebrand is a Professor of Philosophy at the University of Colorado Denver and served as Chair from 2013–2021. Professor Hildebrand serves numerous national and international academic societies and journals. He is a past President of the Society for the Advancement of American Philosophy and the Southwestern Philosophical Society.
