Our approach

An inquirer's approach to a fast-moving field.

AI is reshaping medicine, education, governance, warfare, and everyday life. We exist to ensure these transformations are guided by careful ethical reflection — not enthusiasm or doomsaying alone.

We promote a normatively grounded, philosophically rigorous, and publicly engaged approach to AI governance — emphasizing transparency, epistemic responsibility, and the role of human judgment in high-stakes decisions.
